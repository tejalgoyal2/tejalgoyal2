Mona Sans (github/mona-sans) and Monaspace Neon (githubnext/monaspace) are GitHub's own typefaces, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
Monaspace Neon was cut to static weights 500 and 700 with fontTools; both families are trimmed to Latin. They are used only to draw outlines (scripts/type.mjs); no font file is served to browsers.
